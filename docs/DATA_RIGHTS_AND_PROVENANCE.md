# Data rights and provenance

This document records what is released, what is withheld, and on what basis. It is a legal-position
summary written by the author, not legal advice. Nothing below should be read as a representation
that the author holds rights they do not hold.

## 1. Provenance of the radar data

All radar measurements are from the **IPIX** X-band polarimetric sea-clutter database (Ice
Multiparameter Imaging X-band radar), a third-party experimental dataset originally recorded by the
Defence Research Establishment Ottawa with the Radar Systems Group, and distributed on CD-ROM as a
public research database. The author obtained it as the standard distribution media and did not
record any of the data.

The study uses 16 recordings: 14 `starea`/`stareC0000` acquisitions from 1993 and 2 `ANTSTEP`
acquisitions from 1998. Each is a dwell (staring) recording with four polarisation channels
(HH, HV, VH, VV), a 1000 Hz pulse repetition frequency, and either 14 range cells at 131,072 pulses
(1993) or 28 range cells at 60,000 pulses (1998). The exact list, the target cells excluded from
every evaluation cell in each recording, and the authority for each exclusion are in
`../metadata/target_cells.json` and `../protocol/DATA_PARTITION_MANIFEST.csv`.

**The recordings are not the author's property and are not redistributed in any form by this
release.**

## 2. Classification and decisions

| class | examples | rights position | decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A — original third-party raw data** | `*.cdf`, `*.CDF`, the 11 `IPIX_CD*.ISO` images | Third-party research data; the author holds a copy, not redistribution rights | **WITHHELD.** Never uploaded. Obtained by the user from the distributor (see `DATA_DOWNLOAD_INSTRUCTIONS.md`). |
| **B — derived data** | `*_cubes.npz` complex cubes, any extracted windows or normalised copies | A derived copy of A. Generation does not create a new right to redistribute the underlying measurements, and these cubes are near-lossless | **WITHHELD.** `data/README.md` explains how to build them locally. `.gitignore` blocks them. |
| **C — original experiment outputs** | the 398 campaign score arrays, calibration preparations, released tables, statistics, figure data | The author's own measurements and reductions. They encode detector responses and injected-target outcomes, not the recordings themselves | **RELEASED** in the archival record, with the attribution required by the data's research-use terms. |
| **D — synthetic data and generators** | `src/data/synth.py` (compound-Gaussian clutter, Swerling targets), `scripts/smoke_test.py` fixtures | Entirely the author's work; no third-party content | **RELEASED** under the repository licence (see `../LICENSE`). |

### Why derived cubes are class B and not class C

The cubes are the recordings resampled into complex baseband, not a statistic computed from them. A
third party holding the cubes holds essentially the measurement, so releasing them would be
redistribution of the dataset by another name. Released **scores** are different: they are scalar
statistics of detector outputs on windows, and cannot be inverted to recover the clutter waveform.
That distinction is the reason the results archive is public and the cube cache is not.

## 3. Checkpoint provenance

The nine trained weight files are the author's own, produced by training on **synthetic**
compound-Gaussian clutter generated by `src/data/synth.py`. No IPIX recording enters training, so the
weights contain no third-party measurement and can be redistributed by the author. Two of the nine
(`best_s0.pt`, `best_s0_nophys.pt`) are released; see `../manifests/CHECKPOINT_INVENTORY.csv` for the
release decision on each file.

## 4. What the manuscript may say

The data-availability statement must match this document. At submission time:

* the IPIX recordings are available from their original distributor, not from the authors;
* the released code and results are not yet publicly linked (the archive is prepared, not published);
* the cube cache is available from the authors on reasonable request, subject to the data's terms —
  and this offer must not be written as if it were an unrestricted public release.

`../SIVP_SUBMISSION/.../DATA_AVAILABILITY_DRAFT.md` holds the statement to insert once the archive
has an actual DOI; the pre-release wording is in the submission package as well.

## 5. Third-party software and templates

* The manuscript uses Springer Nature's `sn-jnl` class, supplied by the journal. It is included in
  the submission ZIP because the journal requires it, and is **not** relicensed by this repository.
* Python dependencies keep their own licences (NumPy BSD-3, SciPy BSD-3, PyTorch BSD-3,
  Matplotlib PSF-based, PyYAML MIT, pytest MIT, Pillow HPND). None is vendored here.
* No code from another research group is included. The detector implementations are the author's,
  built from the published descriptions cited in the manuscript.

## 6. Personal and institutional data

The release contains the author's name, affiliation and public corresponding e-mail address, which
the author has authorised for publication. It contains no other personal data, no credentials, no
absolute workstation paths, and no material from unrelated projects. `D_Validation/SECURITY_SCAN.md`
in the final release tree records the scan that verified this.

## 7. If the rights position changes

If the IPIX distributor grants explicit redistribution permission, the cube cache may be added to a
future archive version, and this file, `data/README.md` and the manuscript's data statement must be
updated together. No such permission exists at `v1.0.0`.
